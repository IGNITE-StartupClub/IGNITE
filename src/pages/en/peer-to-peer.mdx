# Peer-to-Peer Learnings

<p class="lead">The Ignite Startup Club gives you the chance to share your experience and skills with other students. In our Peer-to-Peer Learnings you can run workshops or talks that we organize and support with our network, a perfect opportunity to make your knowledge visible and make valuable contacts.</p>

## Become a mentor, speaker or workshop host

Do you have special knowledge, practical experience or useful skills around entrepreneurship or startups that you would like to share with other students? That is exactly what our Peer-to-Peer Learnings are for. Ignite supports you in preparing, planning and running your own workshop, talk or discussion round. We give you a platform to make your know-how visible and, at the same time, to enrich the university's startup community.

Our events are based on the conviction that the most effective way to share knowledge is to learn from each other. That is why, at Ignite, every member who wants to share their experience authentically and practically is at the center.

## Why Peer-to-Peer Learnings are especially valuable

Our Peer-to-Peer sessions are more than a stage for talks. They create space for real exchange and lasting connections. You get the chance to pass your knowledge directly to others, gather valuable experience and further develop your communication skills.

Ignite takes care of the organization and makes sure your event is well prepared and run. You get access to our network, support with communication and an active community that values and discusses your content.

## Which topics are a good fit?

The topics are diverse and always welcome, as long as they help students succeed on their way into entrepreneurship. We already have a few new ideas for you:

| Topic | Focus | What the community gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founding alongside your studies | Time management, prioritization | Practical tips for everyday life as a founder who is also a student |
| Growth hacking for startups | Low-cost reach strategies | New ways to grow on a small budget |
| Personal branding for founders | Online visibility, building trust | Positioning yourself as a founder in public |
| Legal basics (not legal advice) | Legal forms, data protection, founding documents | Orientation in the legal framework |
| Impact & sustainability in everyday startup life | Purpose-driven business, ESG principles | Basics for sustainable business models |
| Tools & productivity for founders | Digital tools, workflow optimization | Tools you can apply right away to work more efficiently |
| Team dynamics & conflict management | Communication, role allocation, conflict resolution | Better collaboration and less friction in the founding team |
| Creativity techniques for finding ideas | Design thinking, brainstorming, ideation | Methods for developing ideas in a structured way |
| Intercultural founding | International teams and markets | Sharing experience from a global context |
| Failure culture & learnings from failed projects | Reflection, open communication about mistakes | Courage and mindset for dealing with setbacks |

## How IGNITE supports your Peer-to-Peer session

IGNITE is at your side from start to finish. When you offer a Peer-to-Peer session, you get:

- **Organization and infrastructure**: We take care of rooms, technology and logistics.
- **Communication and reach**: Your workshop or talk is actively promoted and reaches a wider audience.
- **Access to the network**: Benefit from connections within the startup community and receive valuable feedback.
- **Feedback and coaching**: If you need it, we support you in preparing and designing your session.

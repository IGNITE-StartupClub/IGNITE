## Use of cookies

The term "cookies" refers to functions that store information on users' devices and read it from them. Cookies can also be used for different purposes, for example to ensure the functionality, security and convenience of online services and to create analyses of visitor flows. We use cookies in accordance with the legal provisions. Where necessary, we obtain users' consent in advance. If consent is not required, we rely on our legitimate interests.

### Storage period:
With regard to the storage period, the following types of cookies are distinguished:

- **Temporary cookies (also: session cookies):** Temporary cookies are deleted at the latest after a user has left an online service and closed their device (e.g. browser or mobile application).
- **Permanent cookies:** Permanent cookies remain stored even after the device is closed. For example, the login status can be saved and preferred content displayed directly when the user visits a website again.

## Members area

IGNITE members sign in to the members area of the website with their email address and a password of their choice. An IGNITE admin adds the address, and we send an invitation by email. To set the first password or replace a forgotten one, the member requests a six-digit code on the sign-in page, which we send to the registered address. The code is valid for 10 minutes, works only once and stops working after five wrong entries.

We do not store the password in plain text, only as a salted hash (scrypt, additionally keyed with a secret server key), from which the password cannot be recovered. After five wrong passwords in a row we lock sign-in for 15 minutes. To protect against trying passwords across many accounts, we also store a pseudonym of the IP address for 15 minutes after a failed sign-in (a hash formed with a secret key, not the address itself).

After sign-in we set the cookie "ignite_member". It holds a random identifier that lets us recognise the sign-in and is technically necessary for the members area. It is deleted on sign-out and becomes invalid after 30 days at the latest.

We log changes to the member list (adding, removing, changing a role, sending an invitation) with the time, the affected address and the address of the admin who made the change.

For the optional email signature, a member can upload a photo. The image section is chosen in the browser; only this square section (192 × 192 pixels) is sent to us and stored. So that recipients of the signature can see the photo, it can be loaded without sign-in through a random link that cannot be guessed.

- **Data processed:** email address, role (member or admin), password hash, time of joining and of the last password change, number of failed attempts, pseudonym of the IP address after a failed sign-in, session identifier and codes (stored only as hashes), log of changes, voluntarily uploaded signature photo.
- **Purpose:** access to internal content for members only, protection of the accounts against unauthorised access, traceability of changes to the member list, photo in the email signature.
- **Storage period:** codes are deleted one hour after they were sent, IP pseudonyms after 15 minutes, sessions after 30 days or on sign-out, log entries after one year. We delete the signature photo as soon as the member removes or replaces it or the membership ends. Email address, role and password hash stay stored until the membership ends or the member asks for their deletion.
- **Legal bases:** performance of a contract and pre-contractual enquiries (Art. 6 (1) sentence 1 lit. b) GDPR); for the lock after failed attempts, the IP pseudonyms and the change log our legitimate interest in secure accounts and traceable changes (Art. 6 (1) sentence 1 lit. f) GDPR); for the signature photo the member's consent (Art. 6 (1) sentence 1 lit. a) GDPR), which they can withdraw at any time by removing the photo; the cookie is strictly necessary (Section 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG).

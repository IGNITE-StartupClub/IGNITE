# A new chapter for IGNITE: our board change 🚀

In July, IGNITE held its board change. With Ben Kaiser (left), Laurens Rocker (center) and Marie Grevers (right), our board is getting ready for the new chapter.

Together we want to develop IGNITE further, advance new ideas and create even more room for exchange, entrepreneurship and exciting projects.

A big thank you to everyone who has shaped IGNITE with their commitment so far. We look forward to everything ahead of us – and to achieving a lot together with our team! 💜

# IGNITE x European Mentorship Network: mentoring beyond the campus

We have a new partner: the European Mentorship Network (EMN) connects early-stage founders with mentors from all over Europe. Mentoring is free for founders, and the IGNITE community gets its own space on the platform.

#### What is the European Mentorship Network?

You have an idea or are not quite sure what your next step is? On the EMN you can find mentors by topic, stage and availability and request a session directly.

The mentors join through partner networks from all over Europe. That way you get feedback from beyond your own campus and country.

#### Your own IGNITE community on the platform

As an IGNITE member you get an IGNITE badge on the EMN. With it you can filter specifically for mentors from our community, and IGNITE mentors can find you. So the exchange is international and still close to our network in Lüneburg.

#### How to join

As a member, you get the access code for the IGNITE community directly from us.

1. Sign up with the code at [europeanmentorshipnetwork.com](https://www.europeanmentorshipnetwork.com).
2. Enter the same code under **Profile → Communities** to get your IGNITE badge.

Would you rather mentor yourself? Then sign up as a mentor with the same code.

#### Thank you!

A big thank you to the European Mentorship Network team for the easy collaboration. We look forward to seeing what comes out of the first sessions! 🚀

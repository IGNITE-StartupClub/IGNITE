# New ideas, exciting conversations: IGNITE at Startup Contacts

On 15 June 2026 we were at Startup Contacts in the Halle Münsterland with IGNITE! The fair, organized by Venture Club Münster, was held under the motto "Future through collaboration" and brought together startups, companies, students and investors. For us, the perfect opportunity to get to know new ideas, make exciting contacts and experience the startup scene up close.

#### Startups, innovations and new perspectives

There was a lot to discover at the fair. At the various stands we got to know innovative startups and companies and gained exciting insights into their ideas, products and business models.

It was especially interesting to see the different approaches founders take to current challenges. From first business ideas to established solutions, there was a bit of everything. We not only gained new perspectives but also took away a few impulses for our own work at IGNITE.

#### Networking and inspiring talks

Besides the exhibitors, personal exchange was our main focus. We used the opportunity to connect with founders, students and others interested in founding, swap experiences and make new contacts.

The stage program also had a lot to offer. In exciting talks and discussions we gained insights into the world of entrepreneurship, innovative business models and the challenges of building a startup. The speakers' personal experiences in particular showed how important courage, perseverance and the right contacts can be for turning an idea into reality.

#### What do we take away?

For us, Startup Contacts was an inspiring day full of new impressions, exciting conversations and valuable encounters.

Once again it became clear how much can emerge when people with different ideas and backgrounds come together. This is exactly the kind of exchange we want to foster at IGNITE, and we will bring the impulses we gained into our upcoming projects.

A big thank you to Venture Club Münster for organizing the fair and to everyone we got to talk to. We look forward to many more exciting encounters! 🚀
